Medical Disclaimer

Please read this disclaimer carefully before using FlareLog.

▲ Not a Medical Device

FlareLog is a personal wellness tracking journal. It is not a diagnostic tool, does not suggest medication changes or treatment plans, does not recommend activity limits, and is not a medical device.

▲ Personal Reference Only

All content, statistics, and observations generated in this app are derived mathematically from data you enter. They are for informational purposes only, to help you identify statistical patterns in your own journal records.

▲ Consult Your Doctor

This app is not a substitute for clinical advice. You should always discuss your symptoms, logs, and any statistical findings with a qualified physician or doctor. Never disregard or delay seeking medical help due to information tracked in this app.
